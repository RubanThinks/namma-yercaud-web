The Yercaud Experience: Must-Visit Spots & Secrets

Yercaud is nicknamed the "Jewel of the South" for a reason. Here are the top viewpoints and hidden gems you must not miss during your weekend getaway.

Heart of Town

Emerald Lake & Boat House

Surrounded by gardens and towering eucalyptus trees. Perfect for pedal boating in the afternoon and eating hot corn by the shores.

Best time: 3 PM - 6 PM ✦ Family Favorite
Panoramic Cliff

Lady's Seat & Gent's Seat

A sheer natural rock cliff looking over the Salem plains and the 20 winding hairpin bends. Breathtaking telescope views at dusk.

Best time: Sunset & Night Lights ✦ 360° Panorama
Eastern Ridge

Pagoda Point (Pyramid View)

Features stone pyramids erected by local tribes. Known for incredible cool breezes and sweeping vistas of Kakambadi tribal settlements.

Best time: Morning 7 AM - 10 AM ✦ Peaceful Breeze
300ft Forest Waterfall

Kiliyur Falls & Trek

A 300-foot waterfall cascading into the gorge. Reachable via an exhilarating walk down stone steps through dense oak vegetation.

Requires walking / steps ✦ Nature Trek
Highest Peak (5,326 ft)

Shevaroy Temple Cave

The highest point on the mountain plateau. Ancient cave shrine dedicated to Lord Shevaroyan with panoramic 360-degree hill views.

Chilly Winds • Highest Point ✦ Sacred Heritage
Private Estate Walk

Coffee & Pepper Estate Walks

Walk under towering canopy trees wrapped in pepper vines. Learn how Arabica beans are harvested, pulped, and dried in the mountain sun.
